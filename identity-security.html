The Challenge

Modern MFA doesn't automatically protect every authentication path.

Legacy systems, service accounts and privileged access can remain outside conventional cloud sign-in controls.

01 · Legacy

On-premises authentication

Understand how Kerberos, NTLM, LDAP and older applications are accessed, and assess where additional authentication controls are appropriate.

02 · Non-human

Service accounts

Discover service account activity, understand expected behaviour and explore controls that restrict unauthorised authentication paths.

03 · Privileged

High-impact access

Prioritise domain administrators, infrastructure operators and third-party privileged access to reduce credential-abuse risk.

Our Approach

Assess. Prioritise. Protect.

CoreCloud coordinates the commercial engagement and specialist OEM validation to align identity controls with operational requirements.

1. Discover

Map human and non-human identities, critical applications, authentication paths and current controls.

2. Validate

Prioritise material gaps and confirm supported Silverfort capabilities and integration prerequisites with the technical team.

3. Progress

Define a bounded proof of value, deployment approach and commercial proposal with clear acceptance criteria.
